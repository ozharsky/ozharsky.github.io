Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 September 2026.

The short version

Mossy has no account, no analytics, and no servers of its own. Your plants, photos, care reports and watering history are stored on your device and are never uploaded to us. We cannot see them, because there is nowhere for them to go.

What stays on your device

  • Photos you take or choose for identification
  • Plant identifications and care reports
  • Watering history and growth photos
  • Notification and reminder settings
  • Whether you’ve purchased Mossy Pro

Plant identification runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device foundation model. Your photos are not sent anywhere to be identified.

The one thing that leaves your device

To show a reference photo of an identified species, Mossy requests that species’ summary from Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org). That request contains the plant name only — never your photo, your location, or anything identifying you. If the request fails, Mossy carries on without a reference image.

Plant tags

Writing an NFC tag stores a link and the plant’s name on the tag itself, in your hand. Nothing is transmitted. Anyone who taps that tag sees the plant name and its care needs — so treat a tag the way you’d treat a written label.

Purchases

Mossy Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Mossy only learns whether the purchase exists.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no third-party ad or tracking SDKs
  • No location access
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data to sell

During the TestFlight beta

If you’re testing Mossy through TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and basic usage information and shares them with us, along with any feedback you choose to send. That is Apple’s process, described in the TestFlight app. It is the only way we receive anything about your use of Mossy, and it does not include your plants or photos.

Deleting your data

Deleting Mossy removes everything it stored. There is no server copy and nothing to request from us.

Children

Mossy is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, at any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If Mossy ever starts collecting something, that will be stated here plainly before it does.

Contact

Questions about any of this go to ozharsky@gmail.com.
