Where your plants live

Your photos never leave the phone.

Identification, the health read and the chat all run on the device. There is no account to make and no server holding your journal, because there is no server.

No accountNothing to sign up for. The journal lives on your phone and nowhere else.

No trackingNo analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs collecting anything.
