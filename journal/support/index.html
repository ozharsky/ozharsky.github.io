Help

Most answers are below. Anything else, email me — it’s one person, so you’ll get a real reply.

Email

ozharsky@gmail.com — bugs, refunds, feature requests, or a screenshot of something that looks wrong. Tell me your iPhone model and iOS version and it’ll go faster.

What you need to run it

  • Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later for writing, moods, calendar, photos, export, widgets and backups.
  • An iPhone with Apple Intelligence for the AI illustrations and the AI weekly summary. Those two features need Apple’s on-device models, which older iPhones don’t have.

On a device without Apple Intelligence the app doesn’t break — the Generate button is replaced with an explanation and the “Attach a Photo” flow, and everything else works normally.

Common questions

My illustration won’t generate

Check Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri on your iPhone and make sure Apple Intelligence is turned on and finished downloading. The first illustration after enabling it can take a while because the model is still being set up. If the Generate button is missing entirely, your device doesn’t support Image Playground.

I paid, but Pro isn’t unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. That re-checks your Apple Account. If it still doesn’t unlock, make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on, then email me.

What does Pro include?

Unlimited entries (the free tier is three a week), all four illustration styles, multiple variations, photo transform, Home and Lock Screen widgets, PDF export, daily reminders, backdating, the Siri features and the AI weekly recap. It’s a single one-time purchase — there is no subscription.

How do I get my entries onto a second device?

Settings → turn on iCloud sync. It’s off until you do. Entries then sync through your own iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted. Both devices need to be signed into the same Apple Account.

How do I back up or get my writing out?

Settings has two exports: a formatted PDF of your journal, and a JSON backup of everything. Both save wherever you like through the normal share sheet. Your journal is never locked in.

I deleted an entry by mistake

Deleted entries go to a Trash and stay there for 30 days. Settings → Trash to restore one, or to empty it early.

Siri can’t find my entries

Siri reads from an index the app refreshes when it runs, so open the app once after installing or after a long gap. If you’ve set an App Lock, Siri won’t read entries aloud while the journal is locked — that’s deliberate. Try the exact phrase “Read my latest entry in Illustrated Journal”.

Can I lock the app?

Yes — Settings → App Lock turns on Face ID or Touch ID. The journal then locks whenever you leave the app.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: reportaproblem.apple.com. If something in the app is the reason, email me as well — I’d rather fix it.

Found a bug?

Send it to ozharsky@gmail.com with what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. A screenshot helps more than anything else.
