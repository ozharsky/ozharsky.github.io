How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

Write it down

A paragraph is plenty. Type it, dictate it, or start from one of the eight templates when the page feels too blank.

Tap generate

Apple Intelligence reads the entry and draws it. Pick a style, make a few variations, keep your favourite.

Come back to it

Months later the picture is what brings the day back — through the calendar, the mood map, or “On This Day”.

Any

No style constraint at all — the app passes your entry straight through and lets the system pick whatever suits it. Good when you have no idea what the day should look like.

A year at a glance

Some things only show up from a distance.

Tag each entry with a mood and the year fills in behind you. Which months were heavy, which stretch you stopped writing, the week everything turned — none of it is visible one day at a time.

Privacy

A diary you don’t have to trust anyone with.

Illustrations are generated on your iPhone. Summaries are written on your iPhone. There is no account to make and no server of ours for any of it to reach — which is a stronger promise than a policy, because there is nothing to break.

Sync is opt-in and encrypted Turn on iCloud sync and your entries travel through your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted. Apple can’t read them, and neither can we.

Yours to take away Export the whole journal as a PDF or a JSON backup whenever you like. Delete the app and it’s gone — there’s no copy anywhere else.
