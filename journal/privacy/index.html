Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026.

The short version

Illustrated Journal does not collect, transmit, see or sell any of your data. There are no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your entries, illustrations and moods are stored on your device. We have no ability to read them.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • Journal entry text and titles
  • Illustrations generated by Apple Intelligence
  • Photos you attach or transform
  • Moods, favourites, tags and writing streaks
  • The approximate city name attached to an entry, if you allow location access
  • Whether you’ve purchased the Pro unlock

None of it is sent to us.

AI illustrations and summaries

Illustrations are created with Apple’s Image Playground, and weekly summaries with Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. Both run on your device. Your entry text is not sent to us, and not to any third-party AI service.

Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, your entries sync through your own iCloud account so you can use the app on more than one device.

When sync is on, journal content — titles, entry text, illustrations, location names and moods — is end-to-end encrypted. The encryption keys stay on your trusted devices. Apple cannot read this content, and neither can we. We never receive it in any form. Your use of iCloud is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Location

If you grant permission, the app records the approximate city name (for example “Portland, OR”) alongside an entry. Precise coordinates are never stored. Location is never transmitted off your device. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings, and the app works normally without it.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use dictation, audio is processed by Apple’s on-device speech recognition to turn your speech into text. Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to us.

Siri and Spotlight

If you use the Siri shortcuts or Spotlight search, entry titles and text are shared with the operating system on your device so it can find and read them. This stays on your device and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications

Daily reminders, if you enable them, are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications and have no way to reach your device.

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details and receive no personal information from the transaction. The app only learns whether the purchase exists.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. Illustrated Journal contains no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting service and no third-party trackers of any kind. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because we collect no data, we collect no data from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all local journal data from your device. If you enabled iCloud sync, you can additionally remove the app’s data from iCloud in iOS Settings under your Apple Account → iCloud. Entries you delete in the app go to a Trash that is purged automatically after 30 days, and can be emptied immediately in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the revision is published at this URL. If the app ever starts collecting something, that will be stated here plainly before it does.
